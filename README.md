# Hi, I’m Fiona.

I explore **AI, human–computer interaction, and the experiences we can create with them**. I like making ideas tangible through prototypes, stories, and things people can interact with.

Away from the screen, I write and wander through unfamiliar cities.

## A few things I’ve worked on

### Pocket Friend

A playful way to break the ice in person, combining software with physical interaction to help strangers start a conversation. Built with friends at a hackathon, and still a project we’re exploring together.

*My part: full-stack software development and iteration.*

[Watch the demo](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1t8336rE1o/) · [Explore the code](https://github.com/fioluuuna/pocket-friend)

### FirstAid Copilot

A first-aid guidance prototype exploring how to offer clear, step-by-step support when someone is under pressure and unsure what to do.

*My part: the original concept and product specification, with implementation led by a teammate.*

[Watch the demo](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1z2E36LE5J/) · [Explore the code](https://github.com/fioluuuna/FirstAid-Copilot)

### Echoes

Write down how you feel, and find a voice from literature that has felt something similar. An exploration of how words from another time can resonate with the present.

*My part: product concept and presentation.*

[Explore the code](https://github.com/fioluuuna/Echoes)

### Guicheng · 归程

An app about life, loss, and the practical matters around saying goodbye. It explores how difficult conversations and unfamiliar responsibilities might become easier to approach, one step at a time.

*My part: product specification, collaborative development, demo production, and presentation in a four-person team.*

[Explore the team’s code](https://github.com/123bingowinnn/death-education-hackathon)

### 你好，我是孙萌潞（Fiona）。

我关注 **AI、人机交互，以及它们能带来怎样的体验**。喜欢把想法做成可以感受、参与和探索的东西，也喜欢写作，在陌生城市里散步。

### 最近的一些作品

**Pocket Friend** — 让陌生人更容易开始交流的线下社交体验，将软件与实体互动结合起来。与朋友一起完成比赛原型，目前仍在继续探索。我负责软件全栈开发与迭代。

[演示](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1t8336rE1o/) · [代码](https://github.com/fioluuuna/pocket-friend)

**FirstAid Copilot** — 面向急救情境的引导原型，探索人在紧张、缺少经验时，怎样获得清楚、可跟随的帮助。我负责创意与产品设计文档，主要开发由队友完成。

[演示](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1z2E36LE5J/) · [代码](https://github.com/fioluuuna/FirstAid-Copilot)

**Echoes** — 写下此刻的感受，寻找文学中曾经有过相似心情的人，让跨越时间的文字回应当下。我负责产品构思与展示表达。

[代码](https://github.com/fioluuuna/Echoes)

**归程** — 围绕生命与告别事务的应用，尝试把难以开口、也难以着手的事情，整理成可以逐步了解和处理的内容。我在四人团队中参与 PRD、开发协作、演示制作与讲演。

[团队代码](https://github.com/123bingowinnn/death-education-hackathon)
