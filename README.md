## 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a first-year undergraduate student with a deep curiosity about **how people interact with intelligent systems** — especially when they're vulnerable, under pressure, or navigating complex emotional states.

My research interests sit at the intersection of:

- **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** — designing systems that adapt to users' cognitive and emotional states
- **Human-Centered AI** — building AI that people can trust, understand, and effectively use
- **Accessibility & Inclusive Design** — making technology work for older adults, people with disabilities, and those with low digital literacy

I'm currently leading a **Ministry of Education research project** on AI-powered first-aid guidance, exploring how real-time voice and visual prompts affect bystander CPR performance under time pressure. I'm also independently researching **narrative-level privacy preservation** for empathetic dialogue systems.

I'm driven by one question: **How can we design AI that genuinely helps people in their most vulnerable moments?**
